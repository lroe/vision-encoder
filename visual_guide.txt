NUMBERS TO PUT ON SCREEN (all from the paper)
------------------------------------------------------------

- SigLIP 2: 40B image and text samples, about 10 trillion image tokens (Section 2.3).
- Image setup: 224 x 224, 14 x 14 patches, 256 tokens. 14 x 14 x 3 = 588 numbers per patch (Section 2.1).
- Test one: pretraining at 7.4% or 15% of the budget ties the scratch encoder. At 60% it falls below (Section 3, Figure 2a).
- Free pretraining: ImageNet probe 80.4% to 84.4%, image-question score highest after the shortest pretraining (Section 6.1).
- Late vision: image score 42.5 when images start at update one, about 35 when they start at 25%, 50% or 75% (Appendix A.4, Table 11).
- Test two (Table 2): scratch encoder 44.4, encoder-free 39.0, wider 38.6, deeper 38.7, both 38.8. Text-and-chart: 38.2 vs 25.2. Two-layer projection 40.5, hierarchical MLP 35.9.
- Scaling: encoder-free needs about 3x the compute to match the encoder, from 0.6B to 6.4B (Section 5.4).
- Test three (Table 3): every layer 39.4, spread 37.6, last eight 35.1, first eight 45.0. All-token controls: 40.1 and 42.3.
- Midtraining: 1.7B model, 1T text tokens, then 1.33T token-equivalents. Full MoT has 3.5x the vision-only parameters and still falls behind (Section 6.2).
- Speed: one H200, 16 concurrent requests, one 448-pixel image and 256 text tokens each. Encoder recipe about 3% slower to train at 4B (Section 6.3).
- Inkling to Inkling 2 Small: 292M-parameter vision encoder (Section 7).
- Limits: same-data encoders, dense models up to 6.4B, 224 px training, mixture tuned on the scratch encoder (Section 8).
